# Totem (2012)

This “Atlas” has been created to document the presence of peculiar elements inside the tuscanian landscapes; these elements (here called “Totems”) are able to inspire a peculiar sense of wonder into the observer, and in the same time can describe concisely the story and spirit of the land were they are found. The use of a poor-quality film helps to emphasize the symbolical quality of these elements.

The atlas with the location of this totems can be browsed on [Google maps](https://www.google.com/maps/d/edit?mid=1kQ6u0GnAi_1V5q_QpSl6nbyj-Hk&usp=sharing).
